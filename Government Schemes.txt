# Government Schemes

## OVERVIEW

The Government Schemes feature provides information about various government schemes and welfare programs available to the community. It helps users understand the benefits, eligibility, and purpose of different schemes.

---

## Description

Create a government schemes page to display important government welfare schemes and the benefits available to eligible community members.

---

## Features

- List of government schemes
- Scheme details and benefits
- Eligibility information
- Application information
- Education and scholarship schemes
- Healthcare and welfare schemes
- Employment and financial assistance schemes

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Government schemes page is created
2. Available schemes are clearly displayed
3. Scheme benefits and eligibility information are provided
4. Application-related information is accessible
5. Page is simple and user-friendly
