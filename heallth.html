Everyday Awareness

Simple Healthy Habits

Small everyday habits can support overall wellbeing.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink enough fluids throughout the day and pay attention to your body's needs.

🥦

Balanced Nutrition

Include a variety of nutritious foods as part of a balanced eating pattern.

🚶

Stay Active

Regular age-appropriate physical activity can support general health and wellbeing.

😴

Get Enough Rest

A consistent sleep routine is an important part of maintaining overall wellbeing.
