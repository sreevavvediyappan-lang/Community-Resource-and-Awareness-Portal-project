Emergency Reminder

If an emergency is happening right now, contact the appropriate emergency service directly. Stay calm, provide your location clearly, and follow instructions from trained emergency personnel.

What To Do During An Emergency

Simple steps can help you communicate important information clearly while waiting for professional help.

01

Stay Calm

Take a moment to stay as calm as possible and focus on getting appropriate help.

02

Call For Help

Contact the appropriate emergency service and clearly explain what has happened.

03

Share Your Location

Give the responder your address or a clear description of where the emergency is happening.

04

Follow Instructions

Listen carefully to instructions from emergency personnel and follow them as safely as possible.

05

Keep Others Safe

If possible and safe to do so, move away from immediate danger and help others reach safety.

06

Wait For Professionals

Allow trained emergency responders to take over when they arrive.

Be Prepared. Stay Aware.

Save important emergency numbers and make sure your family knows where to find help when it is needed.
