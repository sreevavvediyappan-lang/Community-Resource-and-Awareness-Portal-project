During an Emergency

Stay calm and follow these basic steps.

Important Steps

  • Stay calm and assess the situation.
  • Move to a safe place if possible.
  • Contact the appropriate emergency service.
  • Clearly explain your location and the situation.
  • Follow instructions from emergency professionals.
  • Avoid spreading unverified information during an emergency.
