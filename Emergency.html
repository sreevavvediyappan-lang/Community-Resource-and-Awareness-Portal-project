Stay Safe During an Emergency

01. Stay calm and move to a safe location when possible.
02. Contact the appropriate emergency service when urgent help is needed.
03. Follow instructions from emergency responders and local authorities.
04. Avoid spreading unverified information during an emergency.
05. Help children, older people, and others who may need assistance, when it is safe to do so.
