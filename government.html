Important Information

Eligibility requirements and application procedures may differ for every government scheme.

  • Check the official eligibility requirements.
  • Keep required documents ready.
  • Apply only through official government websites.
  • Verify application deadlines before applying.
  • Never share passwords or sensitive account details.
