How CommunityConnect Helps

CommunityConnect helps you discover useful government schemes in one place. You can save useful resources and keep a simple local record of schemes you want to track.


When you select "Visit Portal", you are taken to the official government website.

Before You Apply

  • Read the eligibility requirements.
  • Check the information on the official portal.
  • Keep required documents ready.
  • Use only official government websites.
  • Follow the instructions provided by the official portal.
