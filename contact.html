What is CommunityConnect?

CommunityConnect is a community resource and awareness portal that brings useful information together in one place.

The portal includes community, government, health, education, environment, and emergency awareness resources.

Can I suggest a resource?

Yes. You can use the contact form to send your suggestions and feedback.
