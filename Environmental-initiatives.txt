# Environmental Initiatives

## OVERVIEW

The Environmental Initiatives feature provides information about activities and practices that help protect the environment. It encourages community members to participate in eco-friendly activities and create awareness about environmental protection.

---

## Description

Create an environmental initiatives page to display information about environmental activities and simple ways to protect and conserve natural resources.

---

## Features

- Tree planting activities
- Waste management and recycling
- Water conservation
- Energy conservation
- Clean-up campaigns
- Environmental awareness programs

---

## Contents

- Tree planting information
- Waste management guidelines
- Recycling methods
- Water conservation practices
- Energy-saving tips
- Clean-up activities
- Environmental awareness information

---

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Environmental initiatives page is created.
2. Different environmental activities are displayed clearly.
3. Information about eco-friendly practices is provided.
4. Users can easily understand and access the information.
5. The page is simple and user-friendly.
