# Education Resources

## Objective
Provide educational resources and learning opportunities for community members through the Community Resource and Awareness Portal.

## Features
- Study materials and e-books
- Online learning resources
- Scholarship information
- Career guidance
- Skill development programs

## Benefits
- Easy access to educational resources
- Supports students and teachers
- Promotes lifelong learning
- Improves community awareness

## Conclusion
The Education Resources module of the Community Resource and Awareness Portal helps community members access learning materials, scholarships, and career guidance, making education more accessible and supporting personal and community development.
